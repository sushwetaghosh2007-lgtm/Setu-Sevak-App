Welcome to Setu Sevak! Please review the options setup below before entering the portal:

  • Essential Services: Quick access to Ration Cards, Voter Identification, and Mobile Updates.
  • Welfare Schemes: PM-KISAN, Housing Subsidies, Pension Applications, and Student Scholarships.
